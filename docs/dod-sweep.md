# Definition-of-done sweep (M7-2)

This file maps every bullet of the two *Test and acceptance plan* sections to a
named check in this repository or to an explicit gap. It cites the plans; it
does not restate them. The plans stay authoritative:
[`nsd-plan-final.md`](../nsd-plan-final.md) *Test and acceptance plan* (rows
`FP-*`) and [`nsd-plan-implementation.md`](../nsd-plan-implementation.md)
*Test and acceptance plan* (rows `IP-*`). `FP-*` has 23 rows (6 under *IR
conformance*, 12 under *New focused suites*, 5 under *Fixtures and
end-to-end*). `IP-*` has 17 rows: its five top-level bullets other than "Add
boundary coverage for:" (`IP-1`, `IP-14` to `IP-17`) and one row for each of
that bullet's twelve sub-bullets (`IP-2` to `IP-13`). `CI-1` covers
*Settled decisions* "CI scope". Total: 41 rows.

A check is a `tests/<file>.rs::<name>` that passes in
`cargo test --locked --no-fail-fast`, a script, or a document in this
repository. A gap is recorded, not fixed: it cites a `[!]` row of
[`implementation-status.md`](implementation-status.md) (a missing private
fixture or archive) or a line of [`deferred-work.md`](deferred-work.md). A test
that passes as "pending" without its private archive is not a check for that
archive's leg and is named as such.
